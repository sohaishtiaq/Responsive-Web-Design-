Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

About His Life

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was born on October 15, 1931, in Rameswaram, India. He became a respected aerospace scientist and played an important role in India's space and missile programs.

He served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007. Popularly known as the "People's President," he inspired millions of students through his speeches, books, and dedication to education.

His life demonstrated the importance of hard work, curiosity, humility, and perseverance. He continued teaching and interacting with students until his death on July 27, 2015.

His Famous Book

Wings of Fire is his autobiography, which shares his journey from a modest childhood to becoming a leading scientist.
